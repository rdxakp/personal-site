Welcome to my corner of the internet

12 Oct 2026 4 min read Notes

For years I kept my thinking in private notes — half-finished, never shown to anyone. This site is me trying something different: writing where people can actually read it.

The reason is simple. Ideas get sharper when you have to explain them. A thought that feels complete in your head often falls apart the moment you try to put it in a sentence someone else could follow. That discomfort is the point.

What to expect here

Mostly essays and working notes. Some of it about learning and technology, some of it about the habits that quietly compound over time. I'm not aiming for a single theme — I'm aiming for honesty.

Write what you'd want to read, and publish before it feels ready.

The rule I'm using

One post a week, however short. The goal isn't brilliance — it's showing up often enough that writing stops feeling like an event and starts feeling like a habit.

If you're reading this, thanks for being here. There's more on the writing page, and you can always get in touch.
